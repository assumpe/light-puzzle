What each piece does

  • Crystal

    Shines a beam of its color in one direction.

  • Altar

    Wants light of its ring's color. A ring that goes only partway around wants dimmer light; the card at the top says how much.

  • Mirror

    Bounces light off its face. Tap to turn it an eighth; drag to turn it freely.

  • Splitter

    Lets half the light straight through and bounces the other half.

  • Filter

    Lets half of ordinary light through. Light that has passed one filter dims more the further the next filter is turned from it; turned across, it lets none through.

  • Colored glass

    Lets only its own colors through: white light through magenta glass comes out magenta.

  • Prism

    Spreads light into its colors: red goes straight, green bends an eighth of a turn toward the rainbow side, blue a quarter.

  • Tilted mirror and deck

    Turns light straight up to the deck above, or down from it. Face it at the light; it turns a quarter at a time. Decks can stack: light climbs to the nearest tilted mirror above it.

  • Gloom seal

    Blocks all light, until light containing its color (shown by its ring) touches it. Then it melts away.

  • One-sided mirror

    A mirror with a dark back. Only its shiny face reflects; light hitting the back is swallowed. It turns a quarter at a time.

  • Switch and gate

    Light of the switch's color lights it, and every gate with a band of that color sinks to let light through. Take the light away and the gates rise again, except late in the Underworld, where a gate stays open once the light has rested on its switch.

  • Stone block

    Stops light.

  • Lava

    Light crosses the lava from island to island; only tall rock stops it.

  • Carried pieces

    Pieces in the tray at the bottom. Tap an empty tile to set one down; hold it to pick it back up.
